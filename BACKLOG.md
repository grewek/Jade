- **Float block (`::: float`, render only)**: `::: float [left|right] [N%]`, the float, `:::`, and the paragraphs after it are its text, with no wrapper or close marker for them. It is a `columns::Columns` with `kind: Aside`, `count: 2` and `tail: true`: the closing `:::` is `separators[0]` (rendered as nothing, like a frame line) and `end_line` is no marker line. `columns::derive(markers, line_count)` bounds the text at the next region's open marker (`tail_bound`) or the document's end; `Columns::cap_tail` then ends it at the first blank line outside code at least `TAIL_BASE + TAIL_PER_LINE * float_lines` lines after the float (16 + 4 per line), so a long note is not one region and no paragraph, table or callout is cut. `DocumentCache::cap_tails` runs after every edit (the cap reads line content, not just markers; `patch_columns` unions the old and new extents into the touched range), and after `recompute_all` once the line index exists. **Why a line budget, not the float's real height**: the extent being a function of source lines keeps the region's lines contiguous and `RowCache` independent per region (the first line holds the height, the rest 0); a layout-derived extent would make line ownership depend on the shaped rows. The cost is a gap under the text when a float is taller than its budget of lines (a very tall image over one-line paragraphs, with no later float of the other side to fill it). Layout is `shape_aside` unchanged: text beside the float at the channel width, cut at the float's bottom, then the whole width. **Stage 1, floats written among the text** (2026-11-21): a float block opening within `Columns::reach` (the same budget) of the last float's `:::` joins its region (up to `MAX_FLOATS` = 3 floats), so `count` is floats + 1 and `separators[k]` is float `k`'s `:::`, `opens[k]` its open marker; the lines of a later float block are in `column_lines(text)` (the hull) but not in `text_lines()`, which the layout walks. `shape_aside` shapes every float at its width up front (`FloatShape`) and puts each down when the text flow reaches the block it was written in (`Floats::place_before`, `Columns::float_anchor`; an aside's floats all at its top, as before): at `max(flow y, the side's free y)`, so on the opposite side it sits beside the text and on the same side it waits below the earlier float (CSS float behaviour). `Bands` is a list of `Placed{side, rows}` (floats on a side never overlap) instead of one occupied-rows value per side; `rows_from` is the next change of band, so a paragraph is cut where any float begins or ends. A float is also the end of the text above it only in the sense that the text keeps flowing; nothing is cleared. The widths of a region's floats come from one `aside_split`, so a side is as wide as its widest float. Not done: a float beyond reach or a fourth float starts its own region, and its text is cut off from the first's. **Step 3, built so far** (2026-11-21): *resize* is the divider grip (`Frame.buttons` is false for a tail: only grips, no `+`/unwrap); a drop calls `set_region_ratio`, which for a tail uses `columns::ops::float_markers` and rewrites only the float blocks' open markers (`edit_region` now replaces just the changed lines when the line count is the same), every float on that side taking the share, as for an aside. *Clear*: `::: clear` alone on a line (blank lines around it, like any marker) inside a float region's text is a hidden marker (`Columns::clears`, up to `MAX_CLEARS` = 3, only those inside the capped text; derived in `derive`'s last pass; the marker and its padding line are skipped by `text_lines`); `shape_aside` moves the flow to below every float placed so far (`Floats::clear_y`) when it reaches one. The flip/unwrap menu is still off for a tail region (`column_menu_state`). Not built: dragging a float to another paragraph. Guards: `columns::tests::a_float_block_*`, `document_cache::tests::float_blocks_stay_as_a_fresh_scan_finds_them_across_random_edits` (600 random edits against a fresh scan), `element::tests::a_float_block_flows_the_paragraphs_after_it`. The autoscroll also takes a caret in a region from its drawn position (`caret_in_column`) rather than the region's top. Guard: `moving_the_caret_in_a_region_keeps_its_exact_height`. Cost: one more shaping of the caret's region per caret move or keystroke in it (release, tutorial note: frames p50 2.4ms, p95 5.4ms).
- **Dragging a divider far enough to stack the region** is reversible: the drag math (`column_drag_percent`) works from the shares in the file and the editor width, not from the previewed layout (which may be stacked), so moving the pointer back brings the columns back. While the preview is stacked a ghost divider (accent line and grip, at `columns::divider_x_unchecked`) shows where it is. A region left stacked shows a columns button in the row of its open marker (`restore_pill`, `paint_stacked_controls`) that sets equal shares (an aside: its default share) via `set_region_ratio`; it is not shown when even those shares would stack (a window too narrow) or while the region is shown as source. What stops a divider early: `MIN_SHARE` (10%), a table's minimum box width in either neighbour, and for an aside the text keeping `MIN_CHANNEL`.
- **Aside (floats with text flowing around them)**, merged from `feature/float-aside`, several floats added on `feature/multi-float-aside`: `::: aside [left|right] [N%]` opens it and is the first float; each further float (up to 3) starts at a `--- [left|right] [N%]` separator; the one plain `---` starts the text, which is last. Floats on one side stack down it with a row between and are as wide as the widest of them (`columns::side_shares`); `aside_split` places them and the text channel between, scaling the sides down together to keep `MIN_CHANNEL`. `shape_aside` computes bands per row (`Bands`: which rows each side is taken, including its air row), shapes each paragraph at the band of its first row and cuts it where a band changes, repeatedly. Grips are one per side (the left first): dragging one sets the share of every float on that side. `+` adds an empty float on the side the last one is not. The single-float description below still holds for one float: `::: aside [left|right] [N%]`, `---`, text, `:::`. It is a second `columns::Kind` of the same region: exactly two parts, the float (part 0) and the text (part 1), `percent = [share, 100 - share]` (share 10..=90, default right 40%). `columns::aside_split` places the float on its side and the channel beside it; the float is capped so the text keeps `MIN_CHANNEL` (560px, about 55 characters: measured, narrower channels leave rows 70-90% full and justifying them opens wide gaps; the requested share is kept in the file, only the drawn width shrinks); it stacks when the float would be under `MIN_FLOAT`. Hyphenation and optimal line breaking would help narrow measures further but need per-language support, so they are deferred. `region::shape_aside` shapes the float at its width, then the text line by line: beside the float (its rows plus one of air) at the channel width, below it at the whole width. A prose line crossing the float's bottom is cut at a row boundary by `DisplayLine::split_at` into two `ShapedLine` entries (`raw` ranges tile the line); only plain prose is cut, a table or callout that starts beside the float stays in the channel as one box. `ShapedLine` therefore carries its own `x`/`width`/`raw`, and painting, hit-testing and the caret use that, not the column's. The grip drags the float's share (`column_drag_percent`, aside branch); no `+`; the menu offers Float right/left on a block and Flip side/Unwrap float in an aside. Up/Down step one painted row (`element::move_by_row`, from the last painted `FrameView` kept in `Editor::last_view`, x kept in `visual_goal`; it falls back to the source-line move when the frame is stale or the target is off the editor), which also covers the cut. Home/End still act on the source line. Outstanding: the text part's row count is only checked against the float's, not estimated before first paint; images as the float are untested.
  - **Syntax**: `::: columns` with an optional percent ratio that sums to 100 (`::: columns 60/40`, `20/50/30`; equal by default), content, `---` between columns, `:::` to close, each marker on its own line with a blank line before and after (without them another renderer reads a `:::` after a table row as a row, and a `---` under text as a heading underline). Inside a region `---` always means the next column; a rule inside a column is written `***`. Content has no prefix, so tables, images, lists, callouts and code stay normal top-level blocks (everything except another columns region). Rejected: `>` callout (a prefix on every line), HTML comments (invisible metadata), per-column fences (twice the markers), weights (`3/2`, means less to a reader). Elsewhere the region reads as plain text with a rule between the parts.
  - **Look**: the marker lines are never shown (the raw toggle, Ctrl+/, shows the source); the parts sit side by side at their ratio, separated by the table-line color. The caret inside does not flip the region to raw, so the layout does not jump on a click. Narrow: when any column would be under 160px the region stacks in source order until there is room. Limits: 4 columns; ratio snaps to 5%.
  - **Layout**: one block. Its first line carries the height (the tallest column, in whole rows) and the other lines take 0 rows, the mechanism folded callouts use (`LineState::hidden`). Each column is laid out at its own width, so the row counting and painting of lines, tables, images, callouts and code take a column width and x offset; this is the main cost, to measure against the 16.66ms bar, including a resize. Spike (release, stress fixture, `pure_row_counts` over all 320K lines as if every line sat in a column of that width): full width 2.6ms, 2 columns 4.6ms, 3 columns 6.7ms, 4 columns 6.5ms. A line is counted once, in its own column, so a region costs one pass at a narrower width (about 2.5x per line, as the 1-row fast path stops applying); a note's regions cover a few lines, so this is not the risk. The risk is plumbing (width and x offset through `LineState`, shaping, hit-testing, painting) and the shaped path for tables in a column, which needs a headless frame bench, not this count.
  - **Caret**: Up/Down move within a column, then continue into the next column in source order (no x-matching across columns); clicks and typing edit the source directly, like table cells.
  - **Phases**: (1) parse, render, click, type, stacking, measured; (2) handles in the table-handles style (`table/handles.rs`-like pure geometry, one undo step each): drag a divider (rewrites the ratio), `+` to add a column, unwrap, drag a block onto another block's left/right edge to form columns, a context-menu "Make columns"; (3) a canvas embed beside text, only after the canvas exists.
  - **Built so far**: `text_editor/columns.rs`, the pure parser (`place_markers` finds the isolated marker lines in a window, `derive` pairs them into `Columns`, `scan` is the two together and the test oracle). Every marker needs a blank line (or the document edge) on each side, sits at column 0 and is outside fenced code; the last open marker wins (no nesting); a region needs a `---`, at most 4 columns and a ratio matching its count, else it is plain text. `Columns::column_lines(k)` excludes each marker's padding blank line. `DocumentCache` owns `column_markers` (all marker lines) and `columns` (the regions), built by `recompute_columns` (after the line roles, which say what is code; 3.2ms cold on the stress fixture) and patched by `patch_columns`: rescan the lines the edit rewrote plus one neighbour each side (a marker needs blank lines around it), splice the markers, shift the later ones, re-pair all markers (few). A keystroke that touches no marker and adds no line skips the re-pairing (`record_edit` char on the stress fixture 1.0-1.2us). The patch's touched range grows to every region (before or after the edit) overlapping the window, because a region's height follows its content. **Step 2 (stacked, built)**: `LineState::column_frame` (`columns::is_frame_line`, a binary search over the regions) marks a region's open and close marker lines; `build_display_lines` gives them `hidden_line` (one blank row, like a fence delimiter) unless the caret is on them, when they show raw. A separator already renders as the usual thematic-break rule, so the region reads as its parts stacked with a rule between. Markers keep one row on purpose: a zero-row line cannot hold the caret, and the zero-row hiding comes with the side-by-side layout and its caret rules. The paint trace shows source text, so only the shaping unit test (`a_columns_marker_line_shows_nothing_unless_active`) proves the hiding, not the harness. Guarded by the randomized patch-vs-fresh test (column fragments in its edit set, a region in its text) and three targeted tests.
  - **Step 3a (built)**: `columns::split(total, percent) -> Option<Split>`: shares apply to the width left after 24px gaps (`GAP`, the divider is drawn in the middle), `None` (stack) when any column is under 160px (`MIN_COLUMN`). Pure f32, no GPUI.
  - **Step 3b (built, off in the app)**: row heights. `DocumentCache::column_width` (`None` = every region stacked; nothing sets it yet, painting does) switches `DocumentCache::side_by_side(line) -> Option<(Columns, Split)>` on. Then the region's first line holds the height of its tallest column and its other lines are `LineState::column_hidden` (0 rows, like a folded callout's body). `row_count_for_line` computes it: each column's content lines (`LineState::in_column`, not hidden by the region) through `state_row_count` at the column's width, summed, max over columns (exact, window-based, so tables and callouts in a column are right; regions are few and small). The pure pass (`pure_row_counts_in`) gives region lines 0 and sends only the first line to `shaped`. `row_owner` maps any region line to its first line in `patch_lines` and `sync_active_line`, since a line's raw/rendered state changes the region's height; `DocumentCache::patch_columns` already reports whole regions as touched. `verify_rows` skips region lines (their rows come from the columns, not from shaping the open marker). 
  - **Step 3c (built: paint, click, switch-on)**: `text_editor/element/region.rs`. `shape_region` shapes each column's lines at the column's width (`LineState::in_column`) and stacks them, giving each line's y below the region's top and the region's rows (its tallest column); painting, clicks and the row correction all use it, so they agree. `paint_region` draws the divider (`Role::Border`, in the middle of each gap), then per column the callout bands and table boxes (`paint_callout_band`, `paint_table_box`, the same functions the full-width pass uses, given the column's line tops), then each line through `paint_line` with the column as its frame (`PaintEnv` + `LineSlot`; the body of what used to be `paint_lines`' loop). `verify_rows` shapes a region before painting and corrects its first line's rows, so the heights are right before anything is placed. A click (`hit_for_position`, the per-line part now `hit_in_line`) goes to the column nearest the pointer's x (the gap splits at its middle), then the line under its y; below a column's text it lands at the end of its last line. **Switch-on**: `DocumentCache::column_width` is set to the editor width only where the rows are rebuilt (`analyze_line_layout`, `rebuild_rows_for` at paint, `Editor::record_edit`'s rebuild), never separately, so the doc and the row cache always agree on the width (setting it each frame made the two disagree by a float and rebuild forever). Measured live (release, `vault/Columns Test.md`, 18 regions): paint trace consistent from the first frame, total height stable, frames p50 0.47ms / p95 0.6-1.4ms (max 47ms is the note-open frame); a sidebar drag between 722 and 1062px rewrapped the columns, consistent throughout; a click in each column landed on that column's line and typing edited it. The stacked fallback (under 160px a column) could not be reached in the live app (the sidebars clamp), only in the unit test. Not built: word-wrap exactness (rows come from shaping, so they are exact in the painted window and estimates outside it).
  - **Caret in columns (built)**: `editor/column_edit.rs`. A region's non-column lines (markers, `---`, the padding blanks) have no rows, so the caret never rests on one (`is_hidden_line`): `move_vertical` runs the buffer's Up/Down and keeps stepping while on a hidden line (the preferred column survives), so Down goes down a column, then into the next in source order, then out; Left/Right and word moves go through `skip_hidden_lines`; Backspace/Delete with no selection do nothing where they would join a column line to a hidden one (`at_region_edge`). Autoscroll (`measure_document`) and IME bounds (`element::caret_in_column`, shaping the region) use the region: autoscroll brings the whole region into view (not the exact line), IME bounds are exact.   - **Region edges (#115, #116, hardening #118)**: a part with nothing but padding between its markers (a hand-typed one) has those blank lines as its content (`Columns::column_lines`), so the file is not rewritten until the user types. `Editor::column_padding` then adds, in the same edit, the newline each side next to a marker needs; the same covers the blank line outside a region once text lands on it. All text input goes through it (typing, IME, paste, Enter, Tab), and `at_region_edge` refuses a Backspace/Delete that would put text against a marker. Down on the last row of a part of a region that ends the note adds a line below it, and Up on the first row of one that starts it a line above (#119, `element::leave_region`; on trial, to be judged by feel; a float block's new line is its text, beside the float; a table's own arrow keys come first). Structure edits keep the caret with its text (`columns::ops::moved_line`) and never leave it on a hidden line, where typing would end the region. A right-click on a blank line outside code offers an empty region. `element/region_fuzz_tests.rs` holds these invariants under seeded real key presses (a selection spanning markers may still dissolve a region: it is plain text editing).

- **Images** (planned, render-only first; vault-local files only, no network): Obsidian's formats, `avif bmp gif jpeg jpg png svg webp`.
